# What a player sees in the demo

This describes the packaged build as it is today. Everything on screen is
generated by the game; there are no bought or licensed assets. The cars and
tracks are fictional, and every vehicle, tire and surface parameter is a
placeholder, not a measurement (see `STATUS.md`).

## Starting

`QuickTurnSim.exe` opens on the title screen: the name, the line "A
physics-first driving simulator for wheel users", and a camera flying
slowly round Ridgeline Circuit behind the menu. Buttons: Drive, Replays,
Controls and wheel, Settings, Quit.

A wheel user's first stop is **Controls and wheel**: assign and calibrate
the wheel and pedals, pick a force-feedback preset and adjust it with live
sliders. Changes are kept per preset. **Settings** has display (fullscreen,
vertical sync, anti-aliasing, shadows, ambient occlusion, field of view),
volume, the starting view, seat height, the ghost and the track map.

## Choosing

**Drive** leads to car and track selection:

- Cars: Tern GT, a front-drive hot hatch with a six-speed manual (also with
  a limited-slip differential), and Halden E4, a dual-motor electric sedan.
- Tracks: Ridgeline Circuit (2.85 km, 38 m of elevation, banked corners,
  kerbs, barriers), Lot Autocross (a 412 m cone course), Quarry Road Stage
  (gravel turning to tarmac) and the Proving Ground (strip, skidpad, kerb
  strip, gravel patch).
- Automatic clutch, automatic gears and traction control can each be
  switched off. The force-feedback preset and wheel are chosen here.
- **Garage...** opens the car's setup for that track: tire pressures,
  camber, toe, springs, bump and rebound damping, anti-roll bars, brake
  bias, the differential (limited-slip car) and the gearing, each within a
  range a club car of the class could run. Setups are saved by name per
  car and track, and every replay and best lap records which one was used.

## Driving

The view starts in the driver's seat (C switches to a chase camera). The
physics runs at 1000 Hz on its own thread whatever the frame rate, and the
wheel's force feedback comes from the steering torque the tires produce.

On screen: speed, gear and engine speed or battery; run time, last and best
time; a live gain or loss against the best lap, green or red; a track map
(N cycles whole track, zoomed, heading-up and off); and, on a track with a
saved best lap, a translucent ghost car (G hides it).

On Ridgeline: distance boards at 150, 100 and 50 m before the corners,
raised red and white kerbs the tires ride over, worn rubber on the fast
line, barriers on the outside of every corner, trees that stop the car, and
the car's own tire marks when it slides. Hitting a barrier or tree thuds
and kicks the wheel.

A lap counts only if it passes every checkpoint in order with a plausible
time and never has all four wheels beyond the road and kerbs; going off
shows "TRACK LIMITS: this lap will not count" at once, and a shortcut
shows "LAP NOT COUNTED" at the line. Neither sets a time.

Keys: R back to the start, P pause, H hide everything on screen, M mute,
F1 tire and force-feedback overlay, F2 telemetry recording, F3 save the
replay so far, F4 the force-feedback meter (with [ ] for gain and ; ' for
minimum force), F5 log the force feedback for the next lap, I the starter, Esc the pause
menu.

With the automatic clutch off, the clutch pedal and an H-pattern shifter
(Controls screen, one gate per gear) behave as in a real car: a gear
forced in without the clutch grinds and stays out unless the revs match,
letting the clutch out too fast stalls the engine, and it restarts on the
starter with the clutch down, or by bump-starting it rolling in gear.

## Pausing and finishing

**Esc** pauses the simulation and opens the pause menu: Resume, Restart
session (a fresh start with the same car and track), End session and see
results, Quit to title.

**Results** lists every counted lap or run with its time and cones hit,
marks the best, gives the best ever for that car and track and the number
of laps not counted. From there: Watch replay, Drive again, Title.

## Replays

Every session is recorded and kept when it ends (the newest 20 sessions
over 10 s). **Replays** on the title screen lists them. Playback
re-simulates the recording exactly; cameras are trackside, chase, orbit
(drag to turn it) and driver's seat. Space pauses, the arrow keys jump and
change speed, the bar at the bottom scrubs, and H hides everything for a
clean screenshot. A replay made by an older build plays with a note saying
from when it no longer matches what was driven.

## What is not in the demo

No other cars on track, no multiplayer, no damage, no weather or time of
day.
